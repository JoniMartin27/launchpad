# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other side is trying to help.

## What is expected

- Give people the benefit of the doubt, especially across languages and time
  zones. Issues here arrive in English and Spanish; both are fine.
- Critique code, decisions and trade-offs — not people.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer. This is a small, opinionated tool;
  a rejected feature is not a personal slight.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind; sexualised
language or imagery; publishing anyone's private information; sustained
disruption of discussions; and any behaviour that would reasonably make someone
stop wanting to contribute.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where you are participating in this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer at **jonathanmartinpaez@gmail.com**, or via
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/JoniMartin27/launchpad/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather not use email.

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. Depending on severity,
the response ranges from a private word to editing or removing content, to
blocking the account from the repository. There is no appeals committee — this
is a one-person project, and the maintainer's decision is final.

## Attribution

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to something one maintainer can honestly enforce.
